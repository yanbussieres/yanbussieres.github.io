Three limits, three layers — and the failures that never leave Convex

@convex-dev/rate-limiter is not a stand-in for Telnyx or Clerk. parentInvitations (10 reservations / minute / clinic) is taken inside reserve, in the same mutation that claims the attempt, before Clerk lookup/create and before Telnyx send. Current HV RDL P0s die on that Convex wiring. Telnyx and Clerk 429s are a later, separate failure and do not appear in these events.

What changed in the reading: the 18:00 / 06:00 “hourly batch” is still not a cron — it is many independent scheduled functions (and Workpool booking jobs) whose dueAt lands on the sending-window edge. The limiter is not “keep us under Telnyx 0.1 MPS.” It is an Ovet clinic cap so a burst claims attempts atomically without turning a later vendor 429 into a spent SMS attempt. The P0 is the limiter document fighting itself (OCC), then runScheduled treating that throw as “eligibility changed.”

1. Send-plane layers (not interchangeable)

Layer What it protects When it fires Who chooses the number
Convex parentInvitations This clinic’s burst; attempt claim stays atomic Inside reserve, keyed by org Ovet. 10/min/clinic. Not HV RDL’s invitation window. Not Telnyx. Not Clerk.
Clerk Parent user / token APIs (account-wide) During preparation, after the reservation is claimed Clerk remaining capacity. 429 here already spent an Ovet attempt if reserve succeeded.
Telnyx Sender / profile send APIs (account-wide) At smsTransport.send Telnyx remaining MPS / spend. 429 here is a classified send failure, not OCC.

Relying on Telnyx or Clerk instead of the Convex cap would mean: claim sending, maybe create a parent, then get 429, then treat that as an SMS attempt. Their limits are also not “HV RDL’s invitation window.”

3. Current production — HV RDL

Clinic hvrdl on kindhearted-blackbird-809. Unattended SMS path. Staff UI is not involved. HV Ovet and HV du Boisé share the same code; they were not in these PostHog events. Window edges 06:00 / 18:00 America/Toronto → 10:00 / 22:00 UTC.

P0

OCC on rateLimits

Every clinic’s invitations, reminders, and booking texts share one org-keyed limiter document. Many jobs write that row at once. Convex retries the mutation four times, then fails (will_retry: false, run_reason: action). That is our limiter fighting itself, not Telnyx or Clerk.

P0

AUTOMATED_SMS_REQUIRES_BOOKING

A scheduled job runs against a visit that is not booked (Pending / proposed, or no time). reserve throws. Same runScheduled catch as OCC.

P1

Reminder rescue fail-closed

Production enrollRecoveredReminders (run_reason: httpApi) requireThats “Visit is cancelled or not scheduled” and aborts the whole batch. One bad id rolls back every other reminder in that mutation. This workspace snapshot of main does not define that symbol; the failure is observed on production.

runScheduled catches any throw and calls suppressScheduled. Occupied limiter and “not booked” are treated as “eligibility changed.”

4. Downstream (still on this Convex wiring)

  • Eligible owners do not get the text. OCC / suppress clears smsDueAt and writes a false smsError. The job is gone. Telnyx is never called. Clerk is never called. No automatic replay.
  • Booking confirmations look successful to the work pool. runScheduled swallows the error, so pool retries do not run. The clinic task that a real send failure would open often never appears.
  • Unbooked visits are noise that still burns the path. One Pending row in the 06:00 batch does not stop other jobs, but it pages as P0 and, if it shares the limiter stampede, adds OCC.
  • Rescue cannot finish. One cancelled / unscheduled id rolls back every other reminder in that httpApi call. Those visits stay without reminderDueAt.
  • Operators get a lying signal. PostHog and smsError say eligibility or config; the visit is often still sendable.
  • Nothing heals old rows. Prototype policy: no backfill. A suppressed invitation stays suppressed until someone sends again.
  • Telnyx / Clerk 429 is a later, separate failure. It only happens after a reservation is claimed. These P0s die before that boundary.
Until this is deployed, HV RDL’s 06:00 / 18:00 batches can still drop real reservations, and the reminder rescue can still fail closed on one bad id.
